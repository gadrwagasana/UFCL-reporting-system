Role Profile — CEO: Cross-workshop role with no workshop restriction. Full visibility across Headquarters, Gatare Workshop, Nyanza Workshop, and Showroom. Read-only on operational data entry modules. Can approve/reject requests via the Changes module. Access to KPI, financial reports, audit logs, and executive dashboards. User management is intentionally reserved for Admin only.
