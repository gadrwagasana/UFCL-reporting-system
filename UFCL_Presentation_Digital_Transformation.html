The Problem

Company Data Is at Risk Every Day

💻 Data Stored Locally

Critical business records — production logs, financial data, operational reports — exist only on individual employee computers with no central backup or protection.

🔥 Hardware Failure Risk

A hard drive failure, power surge, or system crash can permanently destroy months or years of company records. There is no recovery path when data is not backed up.

🚨 Device Theft & Loss

A stolen or lost laptop means company information leaves the building with it. Sensitive business data can fall into the wrong hands with no way to revoke access.

🔒 Knowledge Siloed in Individuals

When employees change roles or leave the organization, the information stored on their computers may be lost entirely or unavailable to their successors.

⚠️

The core vulnerability: Company data that lives only on a local hard drive is one hardware failure, one theft, or one resignation away from being permanently lost. This is not a question of if — it is a question of when.

Business Impact

What This Costs the Organization

Current Risks

Unrecoverable Data Loss

A single hardware failure can erase production records, financial data, and operational history with no way to restore it.

No Management Visibility

Managers cannot see real-time performance across departments because information is scattered across individual machines.

Operational Blind Spots

Decisions are made without accurate, up-to-date data — leading to delays, errors, and missed opportunities.

No Business Continuity Plan

If a key employee is unavailable, the data they hold becomes inaccessible. Operations stall while records are located or reconstructed.

With a Centralized Platform

Data Protected in One Secure Place

All records are stored centrally. A failed laptop has no impact — the data is safe and accessible from any authorized device.

Real-Time Executive Dashboards

Management sees live KPIs, production output, and operational performance across all departments simultaneously.

Accurate, Consistent Records

A single source of truth eliminates conflicting spreadsheets and ensures every department works from the same data.

Full Business Continuity

When someone changes roles or leaves, their work is preserved. Any authorized user can access the records immediately.

Data Security

How We Protect Company Information

🔑 Authentication & Access Control

  • Every user logs in with a unique username and password
  • Session tokens expire automatically after 8 hours
  • Unauthorized access attempts are rejected at the server
  • Admin controls who can access the system at all times

🛡️ Role-Based Permissions

  • Each role can only access data relevant to their function
  • Operational staff cannot view financial or executive data
  • Workshop staff are restricted to their assigned workshop only
  • Sensitive approval actions require senior roles

🗄️ Centralized & Backed-Up Storage

  • All data resides on a company-controlled server — not employee devices
  • A stolen or damaged computer exposes zero company records
  • Centralized storage enables structured, reliable backups
  • Data remains accessible regardless of individual device status

📜 Full Audit Trail

  • Every record creation, edit, and deletion is logged automatically
  • Logs capture the user, role, timestamp, and action taken
  • Management can review who did what and when at any time
  • Change requests require approval before they take effect
