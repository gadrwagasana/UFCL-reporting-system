Why a filled pill instead of just a tinted icon

TreatmentPairingContrast ratioWCAG 1.4.11 (≥ 3:1)
First pass — orange glyph, no background orange icon on navy 2.22:1 below threshold
Existing plain icons, for reference white icon on navy 11.34:1 pass
Shipped — white glyph on orange pill white icon on orange fill 5.10:1 pass
Values computed via the standard WCAG relative-luminance formula against the exact hex codes in mobile/src/theme/colors.ts. The first-pass tint would have made the icon chromatically different but optically weaker than its navy background — the pill fixes that by keeping the glyph itself on a high-contrast fill, independent of whatever the header colour is.
