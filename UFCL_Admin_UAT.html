This package tests the system as two end-to-end production chains, not by module. Each chain is tested top-to-bottom as operators would use it in real production days.

▶ FLOW 1 — TIMBER CHAIN

  1. System Setup: Admin Creates Users & Logistics Registers Equipment (ADM-01 – ADM-08)
  2. Harvesting Operations & Log Transport (ADM-09 – ADM-13)
  3. Sawmill — Timber Production (ADM-14 – ADM-20)
  4. Stock Transfer: Gatare → Nyanza (ADM-21 – ADM-24)
  5. Value-Added Timber at Nyanza (ADM-25 – ADM-31)
  6. Timber Sales (ADM-32 – ADM-35)

▶ FLOW 2 — POLES CHAIN

  1. Poles Purchase Request & CEO Approval (ADM-36 – ADM-41)
  2. Supplier Delivery & Quality Check (ADM-42 – ADM-47)
  3. Poles Production (ADM-48 – ADM-53)
  4. Poles Sales (ADM-54 – ADM-56)

▶ CROSS-CUTTING

  1. Security, Audit Trail & Workshop Isolation (ADM-57 – ADM-63)
  2. Final Sign-Off Summary
