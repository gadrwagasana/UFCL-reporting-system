Welcome to UFCL Mobile v1.0.0

This app replaces paper forms and WhatsApp reports for daily operations at UFCL. Each department records its own data in real time. Data is saved on your phone when offline and synced to the server automatically when network is available. Your login determines exactly what you can see and do — no more, no less.

1. Requirements

  • Android phone running Android 10 or higher
  • UFCL Mobile app installed (get the APK from your IT officer)
  • UFCL network (LAN WiFi — connect to the UFCL wireless network)
  • Your username and password from your IT officer (first login)
  • You can work offline; a network connection is only needed to sync data

2. Login & Sessions

First Login

1
Open UFCL Mobile Tap the UFCL app icon. The splash screen loads, then the Login screen.
2
Enter your username Your username is provided by IT. It is always lowercase, e.g. john.harvest
3
Enter your password Your first password is provided by IT. Change it after first login via Profile → Change Password.
4
Tap Login If correct, you go directly to your home screen. If wrong, you will see "Invalid username or password."

Session Restore

If you close the app and reopen it, you will go straight to your home screen — you do not need to log in again. Your session lasts 24 hours. After 24 hours, you will be asked to log in again.

Tip: If you see the Login screen unexpectedly, your session expired (24 hours). Simply log in again. Your offline data is still safe.

Logging Out

Tap Profile in the bottom navigation → tap Logout. You will be returned to the Login screen.

Always log out before handing your phone to someone else. Each person must use their own account.

3. Offline Mode & Sync

The app works without a network connection. When your phone has no access to the UFCL server, you will see an orange "OFFLINE" banner at the top of the screen. You can still create and submit entries — they are saved on your phone.

How it works

1
Fill in the form and tap Submit The entry is saved locally on your phone. It shows as "Queued" or "Offline" in the list.
2
When you reconnect to the UFCL network The orange banner disappears. Within a few seconds, all queued entries are sent to the server automatically.
3
Verify sync completed The "Queued" label disappears and the entry shows its normal status.
Do not clear the app's data or uninstall the app while you have unsynced entries. Queued entries that have not synced will be lost if the app is cleared.

4. CEO / Admin

Role: ceo · admin

Overview Dashboard

The Overview tab shows live KPI cards: total harvest volume, sawmill production, poles output, active deliveries, pending requests, and team headcount. Pull down to refresh the data.

Approvals

1
Tap the Approvals tab All pending requests are listed: material requests, casual labour, purchase orders.
2
Tap any item to review the details See submitter, date, quantity, reason.
3
Tap Approve or Reject If rejecting, enter a rejection reason. The submitter will see the outcome in their My Requests screen.

5. Harvesting

Harvesting Leader

Full access — can create, submit, and view all harvesting data

Daily Harvest Entry

1
Tap Harvest → tap + (Add) The Create Harvest form opens.
2
Fill all fields Date, compartment number, volume (m³), number of workers, notes (optional).
3
Tap Submit The entry appears in the list immediately. If offline, it is queued and synced later.

Log Transport Entry

Tap Log Transport → +. Fill: vehicle registration, volume (m³), destination (sawmill / poles), date. Tap Submit.

Material Requests

Tap Materials → +. Enter the item name, quantity, and reason. The request goes to Storekeeper and Operations for approval. Track status in My Requests.

Casual Labour

Tap Labour → +. Enter: task type, number of workers, date, notes. The request goes for approval. Track in My Requests.

Harvesting Supervisor

View only — sees harvest and log transport for their workshop only

You can view harvest entries and log transport entries for your workshop. You cannot create new entries or see another workshop's data. If you see data that should not be visible to you, report it to IT immediately.

6. Sawmill

Sawmill Leader

Daily production recording

Record Daily Production

1
Tap Production → + Create Production Entry form opens.
2
Fill all fields Date, logs received (count), logs ready for production (count). The app calculates Timber Volume Expected automatically.
3
Submit Entry saved. Detail view shows calculated timber volume.
The Timber Volume Expected is calculated automatically from logs ready for production. Do not try to type it — the field is read-only and computed by the app.
Sawmill Supervisor

View only — production reports

You can view all production entries in the list and their detail. There is no + button. If you need to correct an entry, ask the Sawmill Leader.

7. Poles

Poles Leader

Production · Purchase orders · Deliveries · QC

Production

Tap Production → +. Record pole sizes and counts for the day. Submit.

Purchase Orders

Tap Purchases → +. Enter supplier, item, quantity, unit price. The system calculates the total and VAT automatically. The purchase requires CEO / Operations approval before it is finalised.

Deliveries

Tap Deliveries → +. Enter truck, customer / destination, quantity, date. Submit. Update the status to "In Transit" and then "Delivered" as the delivery progresses.

QC (Quality Control)

Open a delivery → tap Record QC. Mark Pass or Fail. Add notes if failed. QC result is saved against the delivery permanently.

Poles Supervisor

View only — production and deliveries

You can view production records and delivery statuses. No create buttons are visible. You cannot create purchase orders or record QC.

8. VAT (Treatment Plant)

Role: vat-supervisor

Stock Transfer Intake

1
Tap Inbound tab List of pending transfers — poles sent from the poles workshop.
2
Tap a transfer → tap Intake Enter the quantity you actually received. Must be ≤ available quantity.
3
Submit Intake recorded. Item moves to the Processed tab. Available quantity is reduced.

Processing / Batch View

Tap Processed. All completed batches are listed with date, pole size, batch count. Tap any entry to see the individual records.

You cannot intake more poles than the available quantity shown. If there is a discrepancy, contact the Poles Leader before proceeding.

9. Logistics

Role: logistics

Delivery Orders

1
Tap Deliveries → + Fill: customer name, vehicle registration, quantity, date.
2
Update status Tap the delivery → update to "In Transit" when truck departs, then "Delivered" when confirmed.

Proof of Delivery (POD)

1
Open a Delivered order → tap Capture POD POD form opens.
2
Record receiver name and confirmation Add any notes about the delivery condition.
3
Submit POD POD saved. The delivery record shows POD confirmed with timestamp.

Vehicle Fuel

Tap Vehicle Fuel → +. Select vehicle, enter litres, date, odometer reading. Fuel logs are visible to logistics and admin only.

10. Mechanician

Role: mechanician

Machine Fuel Log

Tap Machine Fuel → +. Select machine from the list, enter litres consumed, date, and machine hours / odometer reading. Submit.

Machine Daily Log

Tap Machine Logs → +. Record: machine, operator name, hours worked today, any faults or maintenance performed, date. Submit.

Record machine faults in the Notes field as soon as they occur — this information is used by the operations team for maintenance planning.

11. Supervisor

Role: supervisor

As a supervisor, your tabs are: Today's Dashboard, Material Requests, Casual Labour, My Requests, Profile. You submit requests for your team and track their status.

Material Request

Tap Materials → +. Enter item, quantity, reason. Tap Submit. Track approval status in My Requests.

Casual Labour

Tap Labour → +. Enter task type, worker count, date. Submit. Track in My Requests.

You cannot see Vehicle Fuel. That screen is available to Logistics only. If you need to report a fuel-related issue, contact your logistics officer.

12. Tracking Your Requests (My Requests)

Every role that submits requests has a My Requests tab. Tap it to see the history of your submissions with their current approval status.

StatusMeaning
Pending Submitted, waiting for the approver to review
Under Review An approver has opened the request
Approved Request accepted. Action will be taken by the relevant department.
Rejected Request declined. Tap the item to see the rejection reason.

13. Common Problems & Support

ProblemWhat to do
Cannot login Check username is lowercase (e.g. john.harvest). If forgotten, contact IT.
Orange OFFLINE banner Normal. Connect to UFCL WiFi. App will sync when connected.
Data I submitted is not showing Check My Requests — it may still be Pending. If not there, check the offline queue (orange banner). Contact IT if the issue persists.
Wrong screen after login Log out → log in again. If still wrong, contact IT — your role assignment may need updating.
App crashes or freezes Force-close the app → reopen. If it keeps happening, take a screenshot and contact IT with the time it occurred.
Forgot password Contact IT. They will reset it. Change it immediately after first login with new password.
